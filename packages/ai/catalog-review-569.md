### Pricing research follow-up (2026-09-23; not a completed S2 disposition)

Primary documents were located by Tavily research and fetched for claim checking; the key schema, model files and provider pages below were also independently fetched. The models.dev `dev` source observed at `7365e7557ad1cb4538346b371cb29fffc3795ec8` ([schema](https://github.com/anomalyco/models.dev/blob/7365e7557ad1cb4538346b371cb29fffc3795ec8/packages/core/src/schema.ts), [Google alias TOML](https://github.com/anomalyco/models.dev/blob/7365e7557ad1cb4538346b371cb29fffc3795ec8/providers/google/models/gemini-flash-latest.toml), [OpenAI TOML](https://github.com/anomalyco/models.dev/blob/7365e7557ad1cb4538346b371cb29fffc3795ec8/providers/openai/models/gpt-5.4.toml)) declares `input` and `output` required, `cache_read` and `cache_write` optional; its authoring [guidance](https://github.com/anomalyco/models.dev/blob/7365e7557ad1cb4538346b371cb29fffc3795ec8/AGENTS.md) lists both as optional. Neither schema nor guidance assigns a price to an absent key. The observed Google alias has `cache_read = 0.075` but no `cache_write`; `openai/gpt-5.4` likewise has a cache-read rate but no cache-write rate. Therefore S1's four-field requirement does not match this upstream schema; absence is **not** source evidence for an explicit $0 price.

- Direct Google Gemini API: [explicit caching](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/generate-content/caching) states "Context caching is a paid feature" with separate cached-token usage and *storage duration* charges; [implicit caching](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/caching) is automatic for supported models. The official [pricing page](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) varies by model and tier, and does not list a separate scalar cache-write-token price for the `gemini-flash-latest` alias. Its explicit cache-creation billing cannot be derived from a missing `cache_write` field; do not mislabel storage as per-token write price or transfer a versioned model's prices to a moving alias. `packages/ai/src/providers/google.ts` reports `usage.cacheWrite = 0` on the direct route, so that field is not presently used to estimate Google cache creation; this does **not** verify a zero provider charge for all forms of caching.
- Direct OpenAI Responses: the [prompt-caching comparison](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/prompt-caching) explicitly says "No additional cache-write charge" for its GPT-5.5 and earlier columns, but GPT-5.6 and later have a 1.25× cache-write token rate. The page also states that write pricing is **not additive**: tokens use either the uncached, cached-read or cache-write rate. This is evidence for the stated direct API models only, not Copilot, Azure, OpenRouter or Codex routes. An omitted write premium for pre-5.6 is not universally equivalent to free input tokens.
- Direct Groq API: its [prompt-caching guide](https://console.groq.com/docs/prompt-caching) says caching on supported models has "no additional fees" and cached input has a "50% discount"; it lists a narrow supported-model set. Do not extend its direct-route capability or price to every Groq catalog entry or an OpenRouter-served Groq endpoint.
- OpenRouter [model/pricing docs](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/overview/models) define `input_cache_write` and say a **value of** `"0"` means free; "Price keys absent from an entry inherit the base price" refers to *conditional overrides*, not missing keys in the base pricing object. The [caching guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/best-practices/prompt-caching) describes provider-specific support and charges but does not assign a meaning to absent base cache keys. Vercel's [REST schema](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/sdks-and-apis/rest-api) defines both cache fields, while its [automatic caching guide](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/models-and-providers/automatic-caching) describes separate capability routing; neither establishes that an omitted price is zero. Top-level aggregate feeds from the inventory above had hundreds of such omissions. Capability, actual cache-token usage, and a price key must be checked separately at the applicable endpoint.

**Local cost-estimation consequence:** `packages/ai/src/types.ts` requires all four cost numbers, and `packages/ai/src/models.ts` multiplies `usage.cacheWrite` by `model.cost.cacheWrite`. The Google adapter always reports zero cache-write tokens, whereas `packages/ai/src/providers/openai-completions.ts` can receive distinct `cache_write_tokens` and removes them from ordinary input tokens before pricing. Thus defaulting every missing write price to zero can understate estimates when an aggregator reports separately billable writes; defaulting to input price can also misstate premium/unsupported routes. The source contract and the runtime usage contract must both be considered before proposing an optional-price policy. This research narrows some direct-route questions but does not establish all aggregator endpoint rates, the Google alias's current target, or the other missing prices. S2 still requires the full candidate inventory, endpoint-qualified evidence and individual dispositions; S3 remains blocked.
